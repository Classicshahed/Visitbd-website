# Design Taste & Anti-AI Frontend Playbook

This skill guides the design and implementation of distinctive, professional user interfaces that feel crafted by expert design engineers rather than generated by an AI assistant.

## Core Directives

### 1. Distinctive Aesthetics & Art Direction
- Never default to standard purple/indigo gradients or generic templates.
- Choose a deliberate visual direction based on product archetype:
  - **SaaS / Productivity (Linear, Raycast style)**: High-density, neutral grayscale backgrounds, 1px subtle borders, high-contrast typography, monospace accents for numbers/meta, command menus (Cmd+K).
  - **Fintech / Enterprise (Stripe style)**: Ultra-crisp gradients, warm neutrals, tabbed panels with interactive graphs, refined micro-animations.
  - **Editorial / Creator (Substack, Readcv style)**: Serif headers, asymmetrical editorial grid, generous line height, paper-like background tints.

### 2. Typography & Hierarchy
- Pick high-caliber typefaces:
  - Sans-serif: `Geist Sans`, `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Cabinet Grotesk`
  - Monospace (metrics, dates, code): `Geist Mono`, `JetBrains Mono`
- Enforce optical hierarchy:
  - Primary headline: Large, bold, tightly tracked (`tracking-tight`), `text-wrap: balance`
  - Subhead: Muted foreground (`text-zinc-400` / `text-slate-600`), readable line length (max 65ch)
  - Micro-copy & badges: Uppercase or sentence case with relaxed tracking (`tracking-wide text-xs font-medium`)

### 3. Surface & Elevation (The Physics of Polish)
- Do not use harsh dropshadows.
- Use layered, diffuse elevation:
  - Ambient shadow + key shadow:
    `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.05), 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0,0,0,0.02)`
  - Card borders: In dark mode, use semi-transparent white borders: `border border-white/[0.08]` over a background of `bg-zinc-900/60` with `backdrop-blur-md`.

### 4. Interactive Feel & Motion
- All clickables must react immediately:
  - Hover: subtle brightness/opacity transition (`duration-150 ease-out`)
  - Active: slight tactile scale down (`active:scale-[0.98] transition-transform`)
  - Focus: accessibility ring with offset (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-primary focus-visible:ring-offset-2`)
